# Tony voice profile

## Preserve

- First-person candor: show the attempt, the mistake, the awkward number, or the changed mind before giving advice.
- Plainspoken transitions: `我觉得`, `你想`, `说白了`, `对吧`, `更准确地说`, `我后来发现`.
- Concrete comparison: price, time, tool, sequence, before/after, or a small scene that makes the idea visible.
- Natural Chinese with occasional English technical words such as `AI model`, `application`, `workflow`, `focus`, `Next.js`, or `API` when they are genuinely how Tony speaks.
- Curious builder identity: try something, observe what broke, explain what changed, and say what will be tested next.
- Mild self-deprecation and honesty instead of expert posturing.

## Optimize

- Keep at most one conversational marker such as `对吧` or `说白了` every two or three paragraphs.
- Remove repeated assent (`对对对`), filler (`嗯`, `就是`, `然后然后`), false starts, and repeated subjects while preserving warmth.
- Shorten long chains of clauses. Favor speakable sentences with breathing points.
- Turn abstract claims into a lived scene or label them as interpretation.
- When Tony changes his mind or corrects a prior statement, keep the correction; it strengthens trust.

## Guardrails from observed samples

- Tony often explains technology through everyday use: payment, navigation, teaching, publishing, building, or a real workflow.
- Tony naturally compares China and overseas markets, but broad national claims must be narrowed to observed products or clearly framed as his current hypothesis.
- Tony's recordings may contain ASR confusions such as `Cloud/Claude/Plaud`, product-name errors, or English homophones. Never silently turn an uncertain name into a fact; add it to `发布前核验`.
- A multi-speaker recording is not automatically Tony's opinion. Attribute the other speaker or omit the point.

## Target sound

The final copy should sound like Tony after one strong editorial pass: recognizably his thought process, more concise, more concrete, and easier for a stranger to follow. It should not sound like a corporate explainer, a motivational template, or an imitation of another creator.

## English voice

- Keep Tony's curious-builder identity, candor, corrections, and concrete examples; do not imitate an American hype creator.
- Use clear international English with native phrasing and moderate vocabulary. Prefer wording Tony can comfortably say on camera over clever idioms.
- Translate Tony's intent, not his Chinese sentence order. `我觉得` may become `What I realized was...`, `说白了` may become `The simple version is...`, and `你想` may become a direct example rather than `You think...`.
- Use contractions and varied sentence length, but avoid slang piles, fake enthusiasm, corporate copy, and textbook transitions.
- Preserve uncertainty naturally: `I don't know yet`, `I may be wrong`, `That's what I want to test`, or `The demo looks good, but that isn't proof`.
- The English version should sound like Tony speaking clearly to one intelligent viewer, not like a translation, subtitle file, press release, or SEO article.
