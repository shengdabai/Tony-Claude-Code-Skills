# Needs Analysis Rubric

Use this rubric to keep verdicts consistent. Scores summarize evidence; they do not manufacture certainty.

## Evidence strength

| Level | Evidence | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Unsupported | agent guess, generic claim, solution enthusiasm |
| 1 | Stated preference | poll, likes, “I would use it”, one anecdote |
| 2 | Concrete past behavior | detailed interview, repeated complaint, manual workaround |
| 3 | Commitment | scheduled pilot, data access, procurement step, deposit, payment, retained use |

Recent segment-specific evidence beats old aggregate evidence. Direct behavior beats proxies. Record meaningful counterevidence at the same level as supporting evidence.

## Verdict rules

### 真需求

Use when all are true:

- the user and situation are specific;
- the underlying problem exists independently of the proposed solution;
- a material consequence or desired progress is observable;
- at least concrete past behavior supports the problem;
- no stronger counterevidence overturns it.

### 有条件成立

Use when the problem is plausible and specific but the segment, consequence, willingness to pay, or recurrence remains unproven. State the exact condition that must become true.

### 伪需求

Use when strong evidence shows one or more of these:

- the request is solution-first and no independent problem can be found;
- claimed users do not experience a material consequence;
- stated interest repeatedly fails to become behavior or commitment;
- a free or existing alternative already solves the job well enough;
- the supposed buyer, user, or triggering situation does not exist as claimed.

### 证据不足

Use when evidence is too weak or inaccessible for the other verdicts. This is a decision state, not a polite synonym for false.

## 广泛 Breadth score

| Score | Anchor |
|---:|---|
| 0 | No identifiable segment |
| 1 | Isolated cases; reach path unknown |
| 2 | Small or fragmented segment with weak reach evidence |
| 3 | Meaningful niche with a practical, repeatable acquisition channel |
| 4 | Several reachable segments or a large concentrated segment |
| 5 | Large, clearly measured, reachable market with converging evidence |

Breadth is relative to the business model. A reachable 500-company niche may be broad enough for a high-ticket service; millions of passive consumers may not be.

## 刚需 Urgency score

| Score | Anchor |
|---:|---|
| 0 | No meaningful consequence |
| 1 | Nice-to-have or curiosity |
| 2 | Useful improvement; action can be deferred easily |
| 3 | Material time, money, risk, or frustration; active workaround exists |
| 4 | Budget, deadline, revenue, compliance, or operational pressure drives action |
| 5 | Mission-critical or immediately costly; buyer is actively purchasing or switching |

## 高频 Frequency score

| Score | Anchor |
|---:|---|
| 0 | One-off or no repeat scenario |
| 1 | Less than yearly or very rare |
| 2 | Quarterly/monthly, or repeat only across different customers |
| 3 | Weekly or reliably recurring in the core workflow |
| 4 | Several times per week |
| 5 | Daily or multiple times per day |

For services, distinguish client purchase frequency from operator workflow frequency. A one-time setup can still be attractive through urgency or breadth, but it should not be mislabeled as high-frequency customer demand.

## Opportunity interpretation

| Passed qualities (score >=3) | Interpretation |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Weak candidate; stop, redefine, or gather decisive evidence before building |
| 1 | Viable with the matching model: broad distribution, urgent premium offer, or frequent retention product |
| 2 | Strong candidate if acquisition economics and evidence quality hold |
| 3 | Exceptional shape, but still test willingness to pay and reachability |

Never let pass count override a `伪需求` verdict. When evidence quality is mostly level 0–1, cap confidence at low regardless of scores.
